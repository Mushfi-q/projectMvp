Step 1: Goal
Why am i making this project?
A: Main project for college
Who is this project for?
A: Students and Teachers
What is gonna make it valuable?
A: It becomes valuable because it centralizes academic management and
enhances learning efficiency through automation and AI support. Teachers can
systematically manage deadlines, assignments, and evaluations in one
platform, reducing manual tracking. Students receive timely reminders,
structured academic updates, and personalized support through an AI chatbot
that understands their semester, syllabus, and deadlines. This improves
organization, reduces missed submissions, enhances exam preparation, and
strengthens communication between students and teachers.

Request Flow Example
How does a student query get processed?”
Answer:
1.Student sends a question through the frontend.
2.Request hits FastAPI chat endpoint.
3.Backend queries the vector database for relevant chunks.
4.Retrieved context is combined with the user query.
5.LLM generates a response.
6.Response returned to the frontend.
